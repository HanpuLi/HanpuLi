# Hanpu Li · 李函璞

Also credited as **Caitlyn Lye**.

London-based writer and researcher working across **film criticism, digital publishing, photography, spatial research, remote sensing and open-source systems**.

I hold a **Graduate Diploma in Humanities and Social Sciences (Distinction)** from **Queen Mary University of London, School of the Arts** (2026), and a **Diploma of Higher Education in English Language and Linguistics** from the **University of York** (2024).

**[Portfolio, writing and projects](https://hanpuli.github.io/)** · **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hanpuli/)** · **[Academia.edu](https://independent.academia.edu/LiHanpu)**

## Selected public work

- **[Poetry Voucher](https://hanpuli.github.io/poetry-voucher/)** — independent artist publication and browser-based publishing project about value, format and circulation.
- **[From Gears to Gasp](https://hanpuli.github.io/writing/trainspotting/)** — revised public film essay on *Trainspotting*.
- **[On Takashi Miike’s First Love](https://hanpuli.github.io/writing/first-love/)** — long-form study of gender, obligation and genre form with reproducible computational checks.
- **[The Material Footprint of Virtual Production](https://github.com/HanpuLi/shepperton-spatial-audit)** — reproducible remote-sensing and planning audit of the Shepperton Studios expansion.
- **[London Chinatown Food & Beverage Analysis](https://hanpuli.github.io/London_chinatown/)** — archived OSM-based spatial analysis and interactive map.
- **[ScopeRail](https://github.com/HanpuLi/scoperail)** — scoped local-execution runtime for MCP clients on macOS.
- **[PhoSignal](https://github.com/HanpuLi/phosignal)** — local-first macOS status light for AI agents.
